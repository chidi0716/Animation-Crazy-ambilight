# Privacy policy
- This extension only runs on the anime video pages of ani.gamer.com.tw (https://ani.gamer.com.tw/animeVideo.php).
- No requests are sent to any webserver, website or api. Errors are only logged in the console of the webpage.
- The settings are stored in the storage of the browser. They are only synchronized to your browser account when you click on "Export to cloud" in the options of the extension.
